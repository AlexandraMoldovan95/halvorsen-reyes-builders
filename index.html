Recent projects

Exterior renovation + landscaping · Denver, CO

Who builds

Two people, one job site team

Halvorsen & Reyes Builders started in 2011, on the simple idea that a client who sees the job site constantly, not just renderings, ends up with fewer surprises at the end. Erik runs execution — he's on-site for every project, from the foundation to the last outlet. Camila handles design and the client relationship, from the first conversation to handing over the keys.

Execution & site

Erik Halvorsen

Coordinates every construction phase, directly on site.

Design & clients
